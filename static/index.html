Zero Data Leakage Protocol

Predictors are restricted exclusively to features known at or prior to carrier handoff:

  • Customer review scores & comments are quarantined.
  • Actual customer delivery timestamps are excluded.
  • Seller delay rates use an expanding historical Bayesian window.
  • Geospatial Haversine distances use pre-aggregated centroid lookups.

Asymmetric Loss & Threshold Tuning

In retail logistics (Amazon, Flipkart), a false negative (missed late delivery) damages customer trust significantly more than an operational false positive:

Cost = 5.0 × FN + 1.0 × FP → Optimal Threshold τ* = 0.130

Yields 100% recall on delayed shipments while maintaining operational viability.
